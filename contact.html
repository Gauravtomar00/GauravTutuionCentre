Frequently Asked Questions

What is the enrollment process at Gaurav Tuition Centre?

The enrollment process is simple. Parents or students can visit our centre, fill out the registration form, and pay the registration fee. We recommend scheduling a prior appointment for a smooth enrollment process. You can also initiate the process by filling the contact form on this website.

Do you offer trial classes before enrollment?

Yes, we offer a free trial class for students to experience our teaching methodology and environment before making a decision. This helps both the student and our teachers assess if the learning style is a good fit.

What is the teacher-to-student ratio in classes?

We maintain a low teacher-to-student ratio of 1:15 to ensure personalized attention for every student. In some specialized classes for board exam preparation, the ratio may be even lower at 1:10.

How do you track student progress?

We conduct regular weekly tests and monthly assessments to track student progress. Parents receive detailed progress reports monthly, and we schedule parent-teacher meetings quarterly to discuss areas of improvement and achievement.

What if my child needs to miss a class?

We understand that students may occasionally need to miss classes due to illness or other commitments. We offer makeup classes or provide class notes and recordings to ensure students don't fall behind. Please inform us in advance whenever possible.
